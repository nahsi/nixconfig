# Research output formats

Adapted from the upstream reference identified in [the skill](../SKILL.md). Match the assignment; these are optional structures, not mandatory report sections.

## Decision brief

- Answer or recommendation within the assigned scope.
- Main approaches and their tradeoffs, with supporting links.
- Discoveries that changed the initial framing, if any.
- Contradictions, uncertainty, and what would change the recommendation.
- Important remaining leads and why research stopped.

## Practitioner or adoption assessment

- Distinguish demonstrated use from interest, promotion, and speculation.
- Attribute first-hand reports with project/version/date context.
- Separate complaints, proposed fixes, and confirmed resolutions.
- Explain the limits of the sample and any inaccessible communities.
- Conclude only as strongly as the evidence allows.

## Comprehensive report

For a user who requests methodology and depth:

1. Goal, constraints, and current framing.
2. Domain map organized by approaches or questions.
3. Findings and comparisons with source-level evidence.
4. Contradictions, alternatives, and consequential lead transitions.
5. Coverage limits, remaining gaps, and stopping reason.

Cite claims where they appear; avoid an unconnected bibliography as a substitute for evidence.

## Continuation handoff

For a coordinator or a later research round:

- Stable goal and any proposed framing change.
- Findings and evidence already checked.
- Compact map of approaches and relationships.
- Open leads, each with an originating URL, why it matters, and the next useful action.
- Rejected branches, access failures, and remaining limits.

Read-only workers return the handoff in their task result. They do not create files, a knowledge base, or additional workers. The coordinator chooses whether a requested durable artifact is warranted.

## Precise lookup

A precise factual lookup bypasses the exploratory workflow: answer, authoritative source, and any version caveat. No domain map or research log is needed.
